Hi! I'm Lydia Kondylidou, a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of
Theoretical Computer Science and Theorem Proving at LMU Munich. I completed my
PhD at LMU Munich in November 2026 (magna cum laude), under the supervision of
Prof. Jasmin Blanchette, with the thesis [*Finding, Simplifying and Presenting
Proofs Automatically*](https://edoc.ub.uni-muenchen.de/37955/).

My research is in automated reasoning and automated theorem proving, including
extending SMT solvers with native higher-order reasoning, minimizing first-order
proofs, and translating machine-generated proofs into readable ones. I also work
on program verification with Boole, the intermediate verification language of
the CSLib initiative, in collaboration with Clark Barrett's group at Stanford
University.
